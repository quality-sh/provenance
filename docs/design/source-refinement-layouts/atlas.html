About this proposal — stance, tradeoffs, what is agreed vs invented

Stance

Ben named a graph as one of the two candidate forms; this is that form taken seriously. The record types are the vocabulary — requirement, decision, rule, question, source — and only real relations join them. Steering means seeing the whole shape and picking any star.

Hypothesis

The person feels detached when structure forms out of view because relations are invisible in prose. A constellation makes the difference between “agreed” and “proposed”, and between refining, resolving and citing, impossible to blur.

Tradeoffs

Reading a paragraph of rationale inside a side panel is more effort than reading it in a document; long statements fragment across clicks. Layout also has to be maintained as the graph grows — the constellation needs tending to stay truthful.

Agreed vs invented

Agreed: both requirements, both approved decisions, the open question, the source and its two verbatim passages. Invented here: the sub-requirement and rule (dashed, marked illustrative), every sample thread, and the “agreed only” presentation idea itself.

Citation

Citation = source + free-text clause, as stored today. Finer passage anchoring is separate future shaping work.
