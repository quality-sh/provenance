About this proposal — stance, tradeoffs, what is agreed vs invented

Stance

The description reads like a text; the argument lives beside it. Every record keeps its margin: status, citations, and the discussion attached to it, aligned to the passage it concerns. Nothing collapses — the person sees statement and dissent at the same glance, which is what “alongside the conversation” can mean at its most literal.

Hypothesis

Detachment shrinks when the distance between a claim and the conversation about it is a glance sideways, not a mode switch or a click chain.

Tradeoffs

The margin forces short contributions and suits two participants, not six. Deep structure (children of children) cannot nest in a margin, so hierarchy is expressed typographically — weaker than an outline or a graph. Narrow screens break the sideways glance; notes drop below their passage.

Agreed vs invented

Agreed: both requirements, both decisions, the recorded page-form question, the source and its two verbatim passages, the detachment locator. Invented: both illustrative records, the sample scholia, and the editing question, marked where it appears.
