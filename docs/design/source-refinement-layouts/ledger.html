About this proposal — stance, tradeoffs, what is agreed vs invented

Stance

Evolution-first. The primary object is the stream of landings: sources attached, requirements minted, decisions approved, questions opened, rules proposed — each with the conversation that caused it. The standing page is a derived view, assembled on demand, every line traced to its entry.

Hypothesis

Steerability comes from seeing what changed and reacting in the moment — the pending entry with Accept / Adjust is the steering gesture. Trust grows because state never shifts silently.

Tradeoffs

A newcomer must read history before content, and “what is true now” costs one click plus assembly. Dense streams need strong filters or they become a queue nobody reads.

Agreed vs invented

Entries 1–6 trace agreed graph events in date order (the two entries sharing one statement is real: the decision resolved the anchor and spawned the requirement). Entry 7 and both illustrative records are invented here; the Accept / Adjust verdicts live in this page only.

Citation

Citation = source + free-text clause, as stored today. Finer passage anchoring is separate future shaping work.
