About this iteration — the correction, the projection assumption, what is invented

The correction

Ben accepted Folio’s document direction and corrected its structure: Provenance already knows what creates what, so the page must nest. The flat buckets are gone. A requirement’s document now holds its decisions; each decision holds what it produced and the requirement it spawned; that spawned requirement holds its own decisions and rules in turn — the creation chain, legible as indentation.

The relations shown

Requirement → refines one requirement, spawned by one decision, cites sources. Decision → resolves one or more requirements, spawns requirements, produces rules. Rule → refines one or more requirements; its producing decisions are optional, so a rule with none nests directly on its requirement. Topic → anchored at its requirement; questions belong to the topic and to the requirement, and link to a resolving decision without owning it.

Design assumption (labelled, not decided)

When a record has more than one parent, this page nests it once, in its creation chain, and shows its other relations as labelled reference lines (“refines … · spawned by …”). Here the shared-view requirement both refines the root and was spawned by its decision — it nests under that decision, with the refinement named on the record. A requirement resolved by two decisions (the shared view, by the editing and reuse decisions) lists both. The alternative — repeating the record under every parent — would duplicate it; nothing here is duplicated.

Agreed vs invented

Agreed, from the coordinator: the root requirement, the five approved decisions, the four spawned or refined requirements, their statements and the page-form question. Invented for this prototype: the illustrative rule, both sample threads, the topic’s second question (source-edit versioning), the projection rule above, and every local edit. The original discovery conversation supports the root; the decisions cite this ongoing session, and the record labels keep the two sources apart.
